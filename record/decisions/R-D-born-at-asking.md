# R-D-born-at-asking — A Decision exists from the moment the question is asked

**Status.** `closed`
**Opened.** 2026-08-23
**Settles when.** Deliberation in progress has a stated legal home; what an open Decision must carry is R-D-unfalsifiable-decisions.

## Question

If a Decision exists only once closed, an argument in progress has no legal home — and it
takes one anyway. Work items swell into decisions in disguise: an argument of thousands of
words stands behind a one-sentence done-when, and the checker reports the record clean
throughout. Where does deliberation live?

## Closed — 2026-08-23

**Ruling.** A Decision exists from the moment the question is asked; an open Decision is the home for argument in progress, and closing appends an event rather than creating the file.
**Scope.** `spec/format.json`, `spec/v0.md`

**Why not the alternatives — each costs more than it holds.** Discipline alone is what produces the disguised decisions,
invisibly: a checker cannot red what has no legal shape to diverge from. A fourth artifact
type adds a lifecycle for something that already has a natural owner.

**What the open Decision buys.** Letting the Decision open as a question dissolves the fork.
Work stays small and boring, which is what makes its word budget enforceable. And a reader
can be told what is settled versus what is deliberately open, which nothing else can offer.

**The cost, stated.** Many Decisions will be open at once. The only defence against “decided
for now” hardening into a permanent state is the written settling condition. Whether that
condition is required or merely customary is R-D-unfalsifiable-decisions, deliberately open.
