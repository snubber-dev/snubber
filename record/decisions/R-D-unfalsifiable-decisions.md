# R-D-unfalsifiable-decisions — Must an open Decision declare what settles it

**Status.** `open`
**Opened.** 2026-08-23
**Settles when.** It is stated whether an open Decision must declare what settles it, or the asymmetry with Work’s required Done when is stated to be deliberate.

## Question

Work’s Done when is required: a Work item must say what finishes it. A Decision’s Settles
when — the same statement, made about a question instead of a task — is optional. An open
Decision without one is the record’s only unfalsifiable artifact: it names no exit test,
nothing reds it, nothing reports it, and it can sit open forever with no way to say whether
it is being neglected or is legitimately unresolved.

The general question underneath — a declared field with real writers and no reader — is not
open here; that is settled honestly per field, and the traversal-not-rule cut is
R-D-evidence-lineage’s. What is open is requiredness alone, and it generalises to no other
field, because no other optional field has a required twin across the type boundary. The
asymmetry either has a reason — a Decision, unlike a task, may be legitimately open-ended —
or it is an oversight this record inherits silently.

The seeding practice is itself a datum: every born-open Decision filed at this record’s seed
carries a Settles when, which is either discipline worth requiring or evidence that the
requirement costs nothing.

This returns at the first born-open Decision filed without a Settles when, or when the 0.2
line opens.
